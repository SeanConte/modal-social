Variance by k

Each agent's judgment is 1 if data-lucky (its best-supported theory is the optimal one) and 0 otherwise. For each run, p is the proportion of agents who are data-lucky.

Social variance (disagreement within runs): the average over runs of p(1 − p).

Modal variance (unpredictability between runs): the variance of p across runs.

Total variance: the variance of every agent's judgment across every run. It equals social variance plus modal variance.
